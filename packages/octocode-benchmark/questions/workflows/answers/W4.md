Near the 5,000 line: werkzeug is the lowest qualifier at 6,886 (+1,886). The highest non-qualifier is `quart` at
3,683 (−1,317): Python, not archived, `requires-python = ">=3.13"` at line 21, and it must not appear. Next is
`itsdangerous` at 3,144. No archived repo is above 5,000 (the largest is `flask-website` at 1,336), so the archived
filter changes nothing at these counts. Ordinary star drift will not change the set soon.

## A good answer must contain

- Exactly flask, click, jinja and werkzeug.
- Each floor taken from `requires-python` on `main`: 3.11, 3.11, 3.10, 3.11, with that line (number and text).

## Optional

- Star counts (approximate is fine: they drift), commit SHAs, the matching mypy and pyright settings, and the
  excluded repos with their reasons.

## Common wrong answers

- Floors read from the latest release tag or PyPI instead of the default branch: flask `3.1.3` `>=3.9`, click `8.5.1`
  `>=3.10`, jinja `3.1.6` `>=3.7` (also jinja's `stable` branch, line 18), werkzeug `3.1.9` `>=3.9`.
- Guessing jinja's floor is 3.11 from the other three repos.
- Including `quart` or `itsdangerous`, or `pallets-eco` repos such as flask-sqlalchemy, which belong to another org.
- Citing a classifier (only the bare `Programming Language :: Python` exists) or the mypy `python_version` line instead
  of `requires-python`.
