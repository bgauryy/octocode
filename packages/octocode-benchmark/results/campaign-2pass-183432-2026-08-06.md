# 3-arm campaign, 2 passes — Octocode vs gh+RTK vs gh+Headroom (25×2, 2026-08-06)

**Bottom line:** Two independent passes (fixed methodology: neutral gpt-5.5 judge, randomized
X/Y/Z, leanest-path enforced, bootstrap CIs). Correctness is a **near-ceiling tie**. On
characters the two baselines behave very differently:

- **vs `gh`+Headroom — Octocode is reliably ~2.9× leaner** (combined geo-mean 2.87×, 95% CI
  **1.98–4.21**, median 3.67×, leaner 36/50; both passes agree).
- **vs raw `gh`+RTK — a wash / high variance:** combined geo-mean **1.21× (95% CI 0.70–2.05,
  straddles 1×)** — pass 1 was 2.22× (Octocode leaner) but pass 2 was 0.66× (RTK leaner),
  because a `gh` runner that answers from tiny `search code` snippets is very lean. **We do
  NOT claim Octocode is leaner than raw gh+RTK** — on current evidence it's parity with large
  pass-to-pass swings.

## Correctness (combined, paired, 50 pairs)

| Pairing | Octocode win / tie / loss |
|---|---|
| Octocode vs gh+RTK | 5 / 35 / 10 (RTK marginally more correct; near ceiling, n.s.) |

Combined means: **gh+RTK 9.66 · Octocode 9.48 · gh+Headroom 8.82**. RTK is marginally
highest because it is a **lossless passthrough of raw `gh`** — the runner always sees the
complete, uncompressed file/diff, so it never misses a detail on the hardest flow/graph
questions (it just pays ~2–4× the characters). Octocode's targeted region reads occasionally
read too narrow a slice there; Headroom's **lossy** compression drops the most. It's a
correctness↔characters trade near ceiling, not a capability gap.

Headroom's correctness fell in P2 (8.44) — it missed several deterministic/flow questions;
RTK stayed highest both passes (9.64–9.68). All within a near-ceiling band.

Bootstrap = 5,000 resamples of the pooled per-question ratios. The RTK CI straddling 1×
means the character result there is **not significant** — it flips with runner path choice
(snippet search vs whole-file read). The Headroom CI sits well above 1× → a **stable** win,
because Headroom's compressor cannot beat Octocode's targeted region reads on large-file /
multi-hop questions.

## Best-on roll-up (2 passes)

- **Accuracy:** three-way tie near ceiling; RTK marginally highest, Headroom lowest (dipped in P2).
- **Workflow:** ~tie (leanest-path enforced held on all arms).
- **Characters vs Headroom:** **Octocode clearly best (~2.9×, CI>1).**
- **Characters vs raw gh+RTK:** **parity / inconclusive** (geo 1.21×, CI 0.70–2.05) — depends on path.

## Honest takeaway

Octocode's durable, evidence-backed advantage is **against compression baselines (Headroom):
~2.9× fewer characters at correctness parity, stable across passes.** Against a *disciplined
raw `gh`+RTK* runner that leans on snippet searches, character use is **comparable** — the
per-question winner depends on whether the task needs a region of a large file (Octocode wins)
or a one-line snippet (gh search wins). Correctness is a wash across all three. This supersedes
the single-pass P1 headline, which over-stated the RTK margin.

Caveats: 2 passes (not yet the ≥3 for tight significance); public contaminated-ceiling
correctness → orientation, not a shipping gate; one neutral judge model.
