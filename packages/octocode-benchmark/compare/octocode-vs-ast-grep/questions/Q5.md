**id:** `ast-grep-q5`

## Question

Across ALL of `$CORPUS/packages` (`*.js`), count call expressions of the
shape `<fn>(<args>)`. Report the count. Report enough completion evidence for
the grader to distinguish a full census from a capped or partial result.

session, report the wall-clock of the single counting command (cold process)
for relative comparison of the two arms. The absolute time is not portable
across machines and is never part of the correct answer — only the count is.
