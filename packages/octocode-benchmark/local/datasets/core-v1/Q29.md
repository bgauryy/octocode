# Q29 — Guava cache weight eviction

**id:** `local-guava-cache-eviction`

## Question

In the Guava clone at `packages/octocode-benchmark/local/repos/java` (main `guava/src` sources, not `android/`), the loading cache is split into partitions with their own weight budgets. In `guava/src/com/google/common/cache/LocalCache.java`: (a) how is the overall maximum weight split across partitions so that they sum to the total (lines)? (b) when a partition is over budget, what happens if the newly inserted entry alone is heavier than the partition's budget? (c) how is the next entry to evict chosen, and which entries are skipped? Give the method names and lines.
