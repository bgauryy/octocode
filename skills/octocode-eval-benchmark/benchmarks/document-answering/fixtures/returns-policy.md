# Fictional store returns policy

## R1 — Change of mind
An unopened item can be returned for a change of mind within 30 calendar days after receipt, including day 30.

## R2 — Defects
A defective item can be returned within 90 calendar days after receipt, even if opened.

## R3 — Missing information
If eligibility depends on information not supplied by the customer, ask for that information before deciding.
