# Optimizer instruction

Improve only the declared mutable subject using development evidence within the assigned budget. First reproduce the failure and identify the earliest divergence. Distinguish a solver defect from an ambiguous task, invalid reference, grader error, or infrastructure failure; verify the deciding evidence and consider an alternate explanation.

State one general failure mechanism, the smallest proposed change, and its predicted benefit. Evaluate the change with the unchanged development harness, positive/negative sibling cases, and existing regressions. Record every candidate, its costs, and the keep/discard decision. Do not add case IDs, expected answers, specific solution hints, or grader-checklist wording to the subject to make known cases pass.

If the evidence implicates the benchmark, report the defect to its owner rather than modifying it during a subject comparison. A harness correction requires a new version and rerunning both arms. Do not inspect sealed-test material, coach an active scored worker, or tune a new candidate from final-test feedback.

Return the diagnosis, evidence, proposed patch, hypothesis, checks and limitations. Distinguish measured development improvement from final acceptance.
