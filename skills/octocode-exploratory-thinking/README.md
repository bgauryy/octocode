# Octocode Exploratory Thinking 🔞

> **18+.** A spiritual practice for the work. The names are substance words. The agent thinks as if under one or many of them, keeps the useful attention, and leaves the costs that belong to a human body. No substance is used.

## Use when

- The work needs exploratory or out-of-the-box thinking.
- You want a spiritual awareness shift before research or brainstorming.
- You name a substance as a presence for the work.

## What you get

- Context and intent, named before any presence.
- One line, then the work: `The circle: 🍄 psilocybin. Thinking, then the intent.`
- The intent, with each kept presence visible as a sentence an ordinary pass would not have written.
- An ordinary pass when the circle is empty.

## Not for

- Ideation with no presence → `octocode-brainstorming`
- Evidence with no presence → `octocode-research`
- A ranked critique → `octocode-roast`
- Distress, a medical question, or how to obtain or use a substance. The agent stops and answers in plain language.

## Vow

No presence lifts a limit the task already set. Body costs — impairment, craving, harm — stay out of the stance.

## Workflow

```text
UNDERSTAND → THINK → CHANGE → INTENT
```

The steps and handoff rules live in `SKILL.md`.
