**Architectural analysis:**
- `honojs/hono` uses a **modular, feature-rich layout** with 10 subdirectories each representing a distinct capability (routing, JSX support, middleware ecosystem, client-side adapters, validators, etc.). The organization reflects a framework designed to be used in diverse runtimes (Deno, Bun, Workers) with opt-in features.
- `expressjs/express` uses a **minimal, monolithic layout** with 6 flat files representing core HTTP primitives: `application`, `request`, `response`, `utils`, `view`, and the entry point. The naming maps directly to HTTP/web concepts without layers.
- The naming difference reveals that Hono is "batteries-included but tree-shakeable" (separate directories for each add-on), while Express is minimalist and relies on a vast ecosystem of third-party middleware to extend its small core.
